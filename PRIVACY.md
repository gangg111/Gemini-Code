# Privacy Policy for Gemini-Code

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how "Gemini-Code" (the "Tool") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
The Tool does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers. All operations performed by the Tool are executed locally on your machine. 

## 2. Google API Data
The Tool uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access your Google account. This is strictly used to authenticate your requests to Google's Generative Language API. We do not access, store, or share your private Google data (such as emails, contacts, or files) unless explicitly required by the functional requirements of the Tool, and even then, such data remains within your local environment.

## 3. Data Security
Your authentication tokens (stored in `token.json` locally on your device) are used solely to maintain your session with Google services. We do not have access to these tokens, nor can we retrieve them.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
